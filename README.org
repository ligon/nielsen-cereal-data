#+TITLE: Nielsen Cereal Data Pipeline
#+AUTHOR: Ethan Ligon

* Overview

Self-contained pipeline from raw NielsenIQ Consumer Panel tarballs to
estimation-ready parquet files for K-aggregator (and BLP) cereal
demand estimation.

* Prerequisites

Raw NielsenIQ Consumer Panel data, transferred via Globus from the
Kilts Center =kilts-panel= endpoint to:
#+begin_example
/global/scratch/fsa/fc_jevons/ligon/Data/NielsenConsumerPanel/
  2004-2020_Panel_Data/Consumer_Panel_Data_{year}.tgz
  2021-Onward_Panel_Data/Consumer_Panel_Data_{year}.tgz
  Master_Files2004-2020.tgz
#+end_example

* Quick start

#+begin_example
make venv              # one-time setup
source .venv/bin/activate

make all               # full 2004-2024 pipeline
make all YEARS="2014 2018"   # just the old sample period

# Or run individual stages:
make extract YEARS="2014 2018" JOBS=8
make crosswalk
make nutrition
make markets
#+end_example

* Configuration

All filtering decisions live in =config/default.yaml=: year range,
product module, number of products, market definition, aggregation
frequency, outside-good calibration, demographics.

* Pipeline stages

1. =src/extract.py= --- extract tarballs, write per-year parquets to =data/raw/=
2. =src/brand_crosswalk.py= --- map brand codes across the 2020/2021 NielsenIQ naming break
3. =src/select_products.py= --- select top-N brands by total expenditure
4. =src/nutrition.py= --- merge FDC nutrition data (=config/fdc_nutrition.csv=) with 2021+ productattributes parsing; manual overrides in =config/nutrition_overrides.yaml=
5. =src/aggregate_markets.py= --- aggregate to =(quarter, DMA, brand)= market-level panel
6. =src/aggregate_households.py= --- aggregate to =(household, quarter, brand)= panel with base-period shares
7. =src/demographics.py= --- market-level and household-level demographic aggregates

* Outputs consumed by K-Aggregators

- =data/clean/market_panel.parquet= --- \((t, m, j)\): shares, prices, characteristics
- =data/clean/brand_crosswalk.parquet= --- maps =brand_code_uc= (2004--2020) \(\leftrightarrow\) =brand_descr_hms= (2021+)
- =data/clean/nutrition.parquet= --- per-brand nutrition (sugar, fiber, protein, fat, sodium)
- =data/clean/household_panel.parquet= --- \((i, t, j)\): household shares, prices
- =data/clean/household_base_shares.parquet= --- \((i, j)\): predetermined shares from base period
- =data/clean/demographics_market.parquet= --- \((t, m)\): market-mean demographics
- =data/clean/demographics_household.parquet= --- \((i, t)\): household-level demographics

Instrument construction (LF, Bartik, GH) lives in the estimation
repo, not here.
